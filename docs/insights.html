    Why tradeoffs matter

    This tool is zero-sum: when residents move the slider toward one service, they are implicitly reducing others. Restricted funds in real life often cannot follow the same moves. Use strong positive signals here as conversation starters — town halls, surveys, and service metrics — rather than as a literal budget line.
